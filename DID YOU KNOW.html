💡 Did You Know?

🌍 Earthquake

  • Can change Earth's rotation
  • Animals sense them early
  • Aftershocks last for days

🌊 Flood

  • 15 cm water can knock you down
  • Floodwater carries bacteria
  • Can occur without rainfall

🌪 Cyclone

  • Has a calm eye in center
  • Eye wall is most dangerous
  • Causes storm surge

🔥 Wildfire

  • Creates its own weather
  • Smoke travels long distances
  • Some plants need fire to grow
